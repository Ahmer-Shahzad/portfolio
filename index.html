P / 02 Final Year Project Oct 2025 – May 2026

Flyboys Warehouse

Built with a project partner: a web customization and ordering platform for a US-based company that makes custom fighter-pilot gear. It replaced their slow, email-based ordering process.

  1. Front end I built the customization and ordering interface with React.js and React Router, including a real-time design preview (Cloudinary) where customers create and see their personalised logo instantly.
  2. Back end I designed the Firebase Firestore database and implemented size selection and direct online ordering.
  3. Impact A faster, more interactive end-to-end workflow, from first design to placed order.
